A version of the shop you can try on your own computer: no installation, no internet needed
after the download, nothing is really sent or paid.

**Download the file for your computer** (under *Assets* below):

| Computer | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 / 11 | `athos-icons-demo-windows.zip` |
| Mac with Apple chip (M1 and newer, most Macs since 2021) | `athos-icons-demo-macos-apple-silicon.zip` |
| Older Mac with Intel chip | `athos-icons-demo-macos-intel.zip` |
| Linux | `athos-icons-demo-linux.zip` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu > About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip,
"Processor: Intel" means Intel.

**Then:** unzip it, open the folder and double-click **Start Demo**. The shop opens in your browser.
The `README.txt` inside explains what to do if Windows or macOS asks for permission, and lists the
demo accounts (password `demo1234`): `maria@demo.gr` (customer), `church@demo.gr` (business),
`admin@demo.gr` (admin panel).

Emails are not sent: they appear on the *Demo mailbox* page, linked at the top of the shop.
